The hero of a poster: a mono over-line, a big condensed Bebas headline, and an optional serif-italic subtitle.

```jsx
<TitleBlock eyebrow="Ce 17 juillet 2026," title="Journée de la Bonne Action" color="red" />
<TitleBlock title="Camp de l'Unité Africaine" subtitle="3ᵉ édition" color="teal" size="lg" fleur />
```

Color the headline red (announcements) or teal/ink; reserve white for teal panels. Let the headline breathe — keep everything else small around it.
