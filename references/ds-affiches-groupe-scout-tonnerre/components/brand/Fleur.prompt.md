Flat fleur-de-lys brand motif rendered from the Unicode glyph ⚜ — never a drawn logo. Use it as a small ornament above a title or in the partner footer.

```jsx
<Fleur size={72} color="var(--teal)" />
<Fleur size={40} color="var(--red)" />
```

Color it with a core token (teal, red, or white on teal). Keep it as an accent, not a hero graphic.
