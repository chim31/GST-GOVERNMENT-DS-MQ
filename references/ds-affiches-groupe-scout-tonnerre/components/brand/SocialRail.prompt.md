The discreet lateral social rail that sits in the poster's right teal gutter — a rotated @handle over a vertical stack of platform icons.

```jsx
<SocialRail platforms={["facebook","instagram","youtube","tiktok"]} handle="@groupesscouttonnerre" />
```

Icons come from the Simple Icons CDN (`cdn.simpleicons.org/<slug>/<hex>`) — standard platform marks, colored white for the teal frame. Pass `handle=""` to drop the rotated text. Keep it quiet; it frames the composition, it isn't content.
