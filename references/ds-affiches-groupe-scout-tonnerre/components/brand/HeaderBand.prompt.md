The brand header band that opens every poster — association over-line, group name in Bebas, and a sub-mention. Lives on the teal frame (light tone).

```jsx
<HeaderBand submention="vous présente" />

// collaboration variant
<HeaderBand
  collaborator="Le Groupe Scout Saint Pierre de Gounghin du Burkina Faso"
  connector="en collaboration avec"
  org="Groupe Scout Tonnerre"
  closer="organisent"
/>
```

Use `tone="dark"` only when placing it on a white/cream panel. Keep the eyebrow letter-spaced and the mentions in mono — they read as institutional metadata framing the headline below.
